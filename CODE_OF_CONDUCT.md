# Code of Conduct

Be kind and constructive. Language learners of every level are welcome here.

- Treat everyone with respect; no harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Critique code and ideas, not people. Assume good intent; ask before assuming.
- Keep discussions on topic and free of spam or promotion.
- Do not post other people's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately (open a private security advisory at https://github.com/avyronn/linguaflow/security/advisories/new, or contact @avyronn on GitHub).
