## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-19

A much fuller Korean pack: vocabulary, grammar and sentence structure. The content was written for the project with AI assistance and has **not** been reviewed by a native speaker (see `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`).

### Korean vocabulary
- Dictionary grew from 1,374 to **9,587 entries** (nouns 5,741 · verbs 1,987 · adjectives 736 · adverbs 591 · interjections 130 · phrases 117 · …). Sources are now split into `data/korean/dictionary.src.txt` + 43 topic files in `data/korean/sources/` (first definition of a word + part of speech wins; a `;` inside parentheses is a build error).
- Numerals and counters: compound numbers (`삼십오`, `스물세`), numbers joined to units (`열두시`, `삼십오분`), and the native numbers 1–4 written together with 32 counters (`두명`, `세잔`) — generated by `scripts/gen-numeral-counters.js` and checked by a test.
- **Hanja + Hán-Việt** for 200 common Sino-Korean words (`학교` → `學校 · học hiệu`), shown on the card; 60 `-하다`/`-되다` verbs show the root word's Hanja (`준비하다` → `準備 · chuẩn bị (từ gốc: 준비)`). A test requires one Hanja per syllable and one Hán-Việt syllable per Hanja (it found a wrong reading of `대학교`).
- Honorific / humble vocabulary (`드시다` = honorific of `먹다`).

### Korean grammar
- Particles 53 → **95 entries**; endings 98 → **341** (197 final · 123 connective · 19 adnominal · 2 nominalizer) plus 138 forms of `이다`; 64 spoken contractions (`우린`, `뭘`, `이건`); 42 auxiliary verbs written together with the main verb (`먹어봐요`, `가고싶어요`); common misspellings are corrected and flagged (`할께요`); spaceless run-ons are split and flagged as guesses.
- **Grammar catalogue (문형): 242 patterns, 509 examples, 25 categories, levels 1–6** (a TOPIK-like estimate assigned by the project) and a small pattern language (`src/languages/korean/patterns.js`). Patterns are matched on the morphological analysis, so `먹고 싶어요`, `먹고싶어요` and `먹고 싶지 않아요` all give `V-고 싶다`; patterns with gaps (`별로 … 않다`) are supported. Every example must be recognised as its own pattern by the test suite.
- **Sentence-structure guide**: 18 topics, 49 examples.

### On the word card
- New sections: **Grammar patterns** (those containing the clicked word, open by default), **Other patterns in the sentence**, **Sentence structure** (kind of sentence, speech level 합쇼체 … 하게체, tense, honorific, negation, question word, role of every word, clauses with their relation, word-order skeleton). Lines that hold several sentences are analysed sentence by sentence ("Câu 2/3 của phụ đề"); quotations are treated as sentences of their own; the main verb of a complex predicate (`먹고 싶다`) is labelled as such. Analysis confidence is shown (*Có thể nhầm* / *Rất không chắc*) with the reason.
- New *Hán tự · Hán-Việt* line (see above). Language-agnostic: any pack can return an `etymology` in `lookup()`.
- The card never covers a subtitle line: it opens above or below the subtitles, wherever there is room, keeps 10 px clear, shortens itself and scrolls inside when it is tall, and re-places itself when you open a section.

### Popup / full page
- New **Grammar** tab: 28 sections, 591 entries (18 structure topics, 189 patterns + 53 endings, 73 particles, 258 further endings); search in Korean or in Vietnamese **without diacritics**, relevance ranking, one section at a time.
- New **Grammar** switch (Home): turn the grammar part of the card off and the card gets shorter; the change reaches open Netflix tabs immediately.

### Fixes
- **Subtitle re-render:** when the player re-drew exactly the same line into a new text node (window resize, re-render) while keeping its container, the cached word map kept pointing at the detached node and hovering/clicking words silently stopped until the text changed. The word map is now rebuilt when the text nodes change. Found by the new end-to-end run; covered by unit tests that fail on the old code.
- **Tall card over the subtitles** (found by the same run) — see "On the word card".

### Tooling and tests
- 111 → **252 unit tests**. `bash build.sh --lint` (syntax check of 75 files → dictionary → tests → packaging → `web-ext lint` 10.8.0 on the files of the extension package): **0 errors, 0 warnings, 0 notices**. Package: 53 files, ≈ 350 KB (static lint only — it does not prove the add-on works).
- **`npm run e2e`** (`tests/e2e/`): runs the packaged zip in a real headless Gecko engine through geckodriver against a fake Netflix page (HTML + NflxMultiSubs-style SVG subtitles, real `<video>`, strict-CSP variant), **46 checks**. Needs Firefox ≥ 140 (or another Gecko browser), geckodriver, openssl and ffmpeg; see `tests/e2e/README.md`. It is not part of `npm test` or CI.
- `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` (provenance of the data and of the tools), `README.en.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md` and the Vietnamese README rewritten for this release; the extension zip now includes `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

### Known limits
See README §3.5. In short: rule-based, not a full parser; 242 patterns are not all of Korean grammar; word cards have no context for homographs; some spaceless/spoken forms can be mis-split (with lowered confidence); not tested on real Netflix, real NflxMultiSubs, stock Firefox, real speech voices or true fullscreen.
